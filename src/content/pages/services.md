I help companies in the Netherlands, Europe and the US build and lead their product and engineering. Through Milan Ventures I offer two things: **dedicated engineering teams in Serbia**, and **product and tech leadership** at Head, VP and C-level.

## Dedicated Engineering Teams

Your team, built and run in Serbia. Milan Ventures employs the people directly and handles everything around them: hiring, contracts, payroll, equipment and day-to-day care. They work as part of your company, in your tools and your rituals.

I have done this before. At BUX I set up the company's engineering hub in Belgrade and grew it from an empty office to a full engineering team in 12 months ([read the story](/posts/bux_belgrade/)). Milan Ventures runs the same playbook for its clients.

### Who we staff

- **Engineers** across all popular stacks: mobile, web, backend and cloud
- **QA engineers** for manual and automated testing
- **Designers** for product and UX/UI design
- **Product and project managers**

### How we work with you

- **Team extension.** One or more people who join your existing team and work under your lead.
- **Dedicated team.** A complete, cross-functional team that works only on your product.
- **Managed delivery.** We take ownership of delivering an agreed scope, and you get one point of contact and regular reporting.

### What you can count on

- **Staffed in less than a month**, from the first call to the first day of work.
- **A two-week guarantee.** If you are not happy with someone after their first two weeks, you do not pay for those two weeks, and a replacement starts on the same terms.
- **Your working hours.** Serbia is on Central European Time: a full working day of overlap with the Netherlands and the rest of Europe, and the morning hours of the US East Coast.
- **Strong English** across the Serbian tech sector ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EF_English_Proficiency_Index)).
- **Screened by someone who has done the work.** I have been an engineer, a product manager, a country GM and a CTO, and I screen every candidate for technical skill and for how they work in a team.
- **One accountable partner.** You work with me directly, not with an account manager.

## Fractional Leadership

For companies that need senior product or tech leadership now, without a full-time executive hire, or before they make one. I step in as **Head of Product, VP, CPO or CTO**.

What I bring:

- **A product organization that works.** Operating model, discovery, roadmap and planning, so teams own outcomes instead of shipping tickets.
- **Tech leadership.** Architecture, engineering practices, tooling and delivery.
- **Teams.** Hiring, structure and onboarding, in-house or with a dedicated team in Serbia.
- **Technical due diligence.** An honest assessment of product, tech stack and team, for founders and investors.

I have led product and engineering at a European neobroker with more than a million users, at a portfolio of AI-first mobile apps, as co-founder and CTO of a startup, and as Head of Product across five fintech and communications products. The [case studies](/posts/) show how.
